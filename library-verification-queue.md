# SnoLab Material Verification Queue

> This queue identifies evidence that must be obtained before a material can be treated as calculation-ready. It intentionally contains no invented engineering values.

| Material | Role | Missing property | Required evidence / standard | Acceptance and calculation impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| غبار السيليكا المكثف (SYS-SCM-SF-001) | scm | pozzolanicIndex | Supplier certificate or laboratory report identifying the SCM type, test age, reference cement/mortar and measured activity index. **Standard:** ASTM C311/C311M (strength activity index); use EN 450-1 for fly ash or EN 13263-1 for silica fume where applicable | Numeric percentage within the schema range, with test age and standard recorded; do not substitute replacement percentage or a generic typical value. Required for pozzolanic SCMs because it affects binder qualification and the engineering warning layer. |
| الرماد المتطاير فئة F (SYS-SCM-FA-001) | scm | pozzolanicIndex | Supplier certificate or laboratory report identifying the SCM type, test age, reference cement/mortar and measured activity index. **Standard:** ASTM C311/C311M (strength activity index); use EN 450-1 for fly ash or EN 13263-1 for silica fume where applicable | Numeric percentage within the schema range, with test age and standard recorded; do not substitute replacement percentage or a generic typical value. Required for pozzolanic SCMs because it affects binder qualification and the engineering warning layer. |
| ألياف فولاذية مستقيمة (SYS-FIB-STEEL-001) | fiber | tensileStrength | Current product data sheet or laboratory tensile test for the exact fiber grade and diameter. **Standard:** EN 14889-1 or ASTM A820/A820M, according to the declared steel-fiber product | Numeric tensile strength in MPa linked to the exact product/lot; do not infer it from steel density, grade name or dosage. Required for FRC/BFUP suitability checks and post-cracking reinforcement warnings. |
| ركام خفيف طيني ممدد (SYS-AGG-LWA-001) | lightweightAggregate | bulkDensity | Bulk-density test report stating loose/rodded condition, moisture state and test temperature. **Standard:** EN 1097-3 or ASTM C29/C29M | Numeric dry bulk density in kg/m³ with the test condition recorded; do not reuse particle density or specific gravity. Required to distinguish particle density from batching/volume behavior in lightweight concrete. |
| ركام باريتي ثقيل (SYS-AGG-HWA-001) | heavyweightAggregate | bulkDensity | Bulk-density test report for the supplied heavyweight aggregate, including loose/rodded condition and moisture state. **Standard:** EN 1097-3 or ASTM C29/C29M | Numeric dry bulk density in kg/m³ with the test condition recorded; do not derive it from specific gravity. Required for mass-volume closure and batching estimates for heavyweight concrete. |
| ركام باريتي ثقيل (SYS-AGG-HWA-001) | heavyweightAggregate | bariumSulfate | Lot-specific chemical composition report identifying BaSO₄ content and the analytical method. **Standard:** Chemical/XRF or wet-chemistry report for BaSO₄; classify the aggregate against ASTM C637 where radiation-shielding use applies | Numeric mass percentage with method, lot and reporting basis documented; do not equate baryte classification with a guaranteed BaSO₄ percentage. Required for radiation-shielding suitability and material traceability; it must not silently affect ordinary concrete calculations. |
| ركام خشن معاد التدوير (SYS-AGG-RCA-001) | recycledAggregate | bulkDensity | Bulk-density test report for the actual recycled aggregate fraction, with moisture and loose/rodded condition. **Standard:** EN 1097-3 or ASTM C29/C29M | Numeric dry bulk density in kg/m³ with test condition recorded; do not use parent-concrete density as a proxy. Required for volume closure and reliable moisture/batching corrections in recycled aggregate concrete. |
| ركام خشن معاد التدوير (SYS-AGG-RCA-001) | recycledAggregate | masonryContent | Constituent-composition report for the recycled coarse aggregate fraction, including masonry particles and test fraction. **Standard:** EN 933-11 | Numeric mass percentage for masonry constituents, with fraction, sampling and test date recorded. Required for recycled-aggregate quality classification and durability/use restrictions. |
